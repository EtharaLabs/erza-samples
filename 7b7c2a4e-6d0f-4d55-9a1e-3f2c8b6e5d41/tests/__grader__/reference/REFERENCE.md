# Reference answer

The copy carried five distinct causes. A claimed cause that describes one of these at a finer grain, in other words, or through one of its symptoms, counts as that cause. A cause may be described by its mechanism rather than by file or function.

1. **Vote requests sent before the candidate's state is durable (asynchronous storage).** With `AsyncStorageWrites`, `raft.send` queued vote responses and append responses behind the durable append but sent a candidate's own `MsgVote` at once, before the new term and the self-vote were written. Violated property: state machine safety (also election safety). Sequence: a candidate campaigns, its vote requests leave while its append thread still holds the term and self-vote; it wins on its peers' votes, replicates an entry `x` to one follower, crashes before its append thread runs, restarts at its old term, campaigns again into the same term, wins, and writes a different entry `y` at the index where the follower holds `x` with the same term; `x` and `y` are both applied at one index.
2. **A snapshot handed to the append thread while earlier committed entries are still being applied (asynchronous storage).** With `AsyncStorageWrites`, a follower that received a snapshot while committed entries before it were still queued on its apply thread handed the snapshot to the append thread at once; when the append thread ran first, the older entries were applied on top of the snapshot. Violated property: each node applies every index once and in order. Sequence: a follower's apply thread holds committed entries; a leader that compacted its log sends a snapshot covering them; the append thread installs the snapshot; the apply thread then applies the older entries again.
3. **Responses released by a Ready that has nothing to write (asynchronous storage).** `needStorageAppendMsg` stopped counting pending after-append messages, and a Ready with no entries, HardState, or snapshot sent those messages directly, so an acknowledgement or vote that depended on an earlier write still in the append thread left before that write was durable. Violated property: state machine safety (a committed entry can be lost; also election safety through repeated votes). Sequence: a follower's append thread holds entries it received; a retransmitted append for the same entries produces an acknowledgement through a Ready with nothing to write; the leader commits on it; the follower crashes, losing the entries; the remaining nodes elect a leader that writes a different entry at that index.
4. **Apply responses that mark every queued batch applied (asynchronous storage).** The `MsgStorageApplyResp` handler advanced the applied index to everything handed to the apply thread so far rather than to the last entry of the batch the response reports. Violated property: each node applies every index once and in order. Sequence: two batches are queued on the apply thread; the first is applied and its response delivered, which marks the second applied too; a snapshot then arrives and is installed even with a hold on snapshots until earlier entries are applied, and the second batch is applied after it.
5. **Append acknowledgements counted as read confirmations.** A leader with pending `ReadOnlySafe` reads counted any accepted append response as an acknowledgement of the newest read position, although the response may have been sent before the read arrived. Violated property: linearizable reads (in both modes). Sequence: a leader replicates an entry; the follower's append response is delayed; the leader is partitioned and a new leader commits and applies a write; a read reaches the deposed leader, the delayed append response arrives and confirms it, and the deposed leader answers with an index below the write already applied.

Causes that must be repaired together: 1 and 3 (both send a message before the state it depends on is durable; repairing either alone leaves the asynchronous-durability violations), and 2 and 4 (a hold on snapshots until earlier entries are applied is defeated while 4 overstates the applied index; repairing 4 alone leaves 2). Cause 5 is independent of the others.

Related facts that are not causes in the copy: data-driven interaction traces under `testdata/` changing after a repair; the race detector reporting nothing; a non-voting leader or a single-voter leader answering reads (both already correct in the copy).

Known observation that is not one of the five causes: the library's `doc.go` permits an application in the default mode to send a Ready's messages while that Ready's entries are still being written. Under that application ordering a follower can acknowledge entries before they are durable. A report may describe this; describing it is neither a listed cause nor a misattribution, unless the report attributes one of the five listed violations to it.
