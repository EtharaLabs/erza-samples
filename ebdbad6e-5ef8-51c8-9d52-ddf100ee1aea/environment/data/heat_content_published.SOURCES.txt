heat_content_published.csv — source and vintage

Annual average heat contents of delivered fuels, by fuel and by the consuming-
sector partition the publisher reports, one value per year.

Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration, Monthly Energy Review,
Appendix A, Table A3 (petroleum products) and Table A5 (coal),
https://www.eia.gov/totalenergy/data/monthly/ — vintage of 2026-07-29.
These series are revised by the publisher over time; figures computed from this
extract are figures under this vintage.

Reuse: EIA grants use and redistribution of its data and requests
acknowledgment. Credit: U.S. Energy Information Administration.
